# StreamRD v0.28.2 — The next season is one button away

## What changes for the viewer
Each season of a show is its own download, grouped under the show. When the last episode of a season finishes, the player now says **Season finished** and offers **Play season 2** (or the next one there is). It goes to the nearest later season that is finished and ready; a season still downloading is not offered. Nothing starts by itself: you press the button.

## Not done yet
Choosing seasons when requesting a show (requests stay one per show).

## Nothing is lost or rewritten
No migration, no new stored data, and no new API: the player reads the seasons of a title from the answer the title page already uses. The player page is now keyed by the item, so a new season starts afresh.

## Upgrading
`docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`, or the **Update** button.

## Verification
- The Chrome journey finishes the last episode of a season pack and checks the card and the button, then follows it to the next season's player page. The lab holds one season, so the second season is added to the title's answer by the test; the player's side of it is what is checked, and the seasons list itself is from the existing title page and its tests.

**Not verified:** playing a real second season after the button (the page it opens is the ordinary player), a season that is still downloading when the first ends, Safari and Firefox, and Docker Desktop, rootless Docker and Podman.
