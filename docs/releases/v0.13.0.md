# StreamRD v0.13.0 — A featured carousel on the home page

## What changes
The banner at the top of the home page is now a **carousel** of featured titles: a big backdrop, the title, year, rating, genres and a short synopsis, with dots, previous/next and pause buttons. It rotates by itself every few seconds, stops while you hover or focus it, and does not move at all if your system asks for reduced motion. Arrow keys switch slides when it has focus.

- **What is featured:** matched titles from your own libraries that have a backdrop come first (up to two), then what is trending right now.
- **Trailers, on your terms.** Slides with a trailer show **Watch trailer** (plays it with controls and sound, right in the banner). Tick **Autoplay trailers** to have each slide's trailer play muted behind the text; the choice is remembered in your browser. Until you do either, **nothing is loaded from YouTube**. Trailers are played by your browser from youtube-nocookie.com, so YouTube sees that request.
- Operators can remove trailers entirely with `HOME_TRAILERS=false`, or the trending lists (and with them the outside slides and trailers) with `HOME_EXTERNAL_LISTS=false`.
- The old single banner is only shown when there is nothing to put in the carousel. The banner colours now follow the black and orange theme.

## Upgrading
`docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`. No database change and no migration; settings and libraries are untouched. Phosphor changes only in its version number.

## Verification
New server tests cover the slides (only backdrop-and-synopsis titles, valid trailer ids only, images only from the allowed host, library titles first, the trailer switch). The component was exercised in Chromium against a production build: no request to YouTube before opting in, next/previous, the opt-in being remembered, the muted background trailer, the Watch trailer player and closing it, and the phone layout. Not verified: that every browser permits muted autoplay in an embedded video, and trailers that YouTube refuses to embed (those show YouTube's own message).
