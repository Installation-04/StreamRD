# StreamRD v0.16.0 — A full-page player, like Plex

## What changes
Pressing **Play from server** on a title now opens the **player in its own page**: black, nothing but the video and a title bar, in the black and orange look.

- **Starts by itself and resumes.** The page asks the server for the stream, shows a spinner while it prepares, and picks up where you stopped (saved every 15 seconds, on pause, and when you leave).
- **Title bar that gets out of the way.** Back button, title, year, rating and file name; it fades after a few seconds of playback and returns when you move the mouse, pause or touch the screen.
- **Packs and shows.** When a torrent holds several videos, a chooser lists them (searchable). While you watch, a **Videos** button reopens it. When a file ends, **Up next** offers the following file and starts it after ten seconds unless you cancel (or press **Play now**).
- **Keyboard:** Space or K play/pause, J and L (or the arrow keys) jump 10 seconds, Up/Down volume, M mute, F fullscreen, N next video, Esc back.
- **Fullscreen** from the top bar or F. The screen is kept awake while playing, where the browser allows it.
- **When a browser cannot play a file** (MKV, DTS and similar), the same help as before appears over the video: copy the stream link or open it in VLC or mpv. Nothing is transcoded.
- Closing the player (back arrow or Esc) saves your position and returns to the title page. The title page no longer carries its own player.

## Upgrading
`docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`. No database change and no migration; settings, libraries and watch positions are untouched. Phosphor changes only in its version number (it keeps its player).

## Verification
The page was exercised in Chromium against a production build with a multi-file pack and a real video file: the chooser, playing a file, Space pausing and resuming, M muting, finishing a file leading to Up next, Play now starting the next file with the title bar updating, closing saving progress and returning to the title page, and no navigation bar on the page. The browser journey that plays a shared title (file choice, playback, saved position, Continue watching) follows the same path and runs in CI. Not verified: subtitle or audio-track selection (left to the browser's own menu), picture-in-picture, casting, or behaviour on a TV browser.
