# StreamRD v0.28.1 — The episode list shows what you have watched

## What changes for the viewer
When you open the **Videos** list of a season pack, each episode now shows where you are with it, as on a media server:
- a **✓** on an episode you finished (95% or more),
- a thin bar under one you began (how far you got),
- the season tab reads **1/3** once some of its episodes are done, and the list opens on the first season that still has something to watch.

The marks are yours alone: another member of the same shared library sees their own. An episode you finish while watching is marked at once, without reopening the list.

## Not done yet
Continuing with the next season's download when one season ends (each season is its own download), and choosing seasons when requesting a show. Requests stay one per show.

## Nothing is lost or rewritten
No migration and nothing new stored: the marks are read from the watch progress that was already kept for every file. The list sent to the player gains `watched` or `percent` on an episode; file keys stay on the server.

## Upgrading
`docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`, or the **Update** button.

## Verification
- A server test: finished, begun and untouched episodes are marked for the member who watched them, nothing is marked for another member, and no file key appears in the answer.
- The Chrome journey finishes episode 1 of a season pack, moves on to episode 2, opens the list, and checks episode 1 is marked, episode 2 is not, and the season shows 1/3; it plays on afterwards.

**Not verified:** the thin "begun" bar in a browser (covered by the server test, not seen on screen), marks for an owner's own library outside a shared library, Safari and Firefox, and Docker Desktop, rootless Docker and Podman.
