# StreamRD v0.31.0 — seasons and episodes on the show page

## What changes for the viewer
- **A show's page now lists its episodes**, the way Plex does: a tab per season (with how many you have watched), a row per episode with its title, a ✓ for episodes you have finished and a thin bar for one you started.
- **Play any episode from the page.** Click its row and the player opens on that file.
- **One button for what to watch next**: **Resume S1·E4** if you stopped in the middle of an episode, **Play next S1·E5** after the last one you finished, **Play S1·E1** on a show you have not started, **Play again** when you have seen everything.
- Opening the page starts nothing: no link is resolved and no watch progress is created until you press Play.

## Not done yet
Marking an episode or a season watched or unwatched from the page, the audio and subtitle choice before playing, shuffle, and the movie page options (versions picker, extras). The page lists the episodes of the season download being shown; a show kept as one download per season still switches season through the Seasons list above.

## Nothing is lost or rewritten
No migration and no new stored data. New API: `GET /api/shared-libraries/:id/items/:itemId/episodes` (the files of a title with this viewer's marks; no file keys or links). The player accepts `?file=` to open one chosen file.

## Upgrading
`docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`, or the **Update** button.

## Verification
- Server tests: the list carries only this viewer's marks, hides file keys, starts no playback and writes no progress; a torrent still downloading says so; a single-video torrent is listed too.
- Type checks for the four apps and the shared-files check.

**Not verified:** the page in a real browser with a long season pack (the lab has no season pack yet), and Safari and Firefox.
